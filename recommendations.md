# Recommendation to Kwame: Franchise Bundles vs. Standalones

**The Verdict:** Favor franchise bundles. 

The data supports moving forward with franchise bundles over standalone hits. While a cherry-picked standalone can yield massive returns (e.g., *Jaws* at 66x), franchises offer a safer, more predictable floor for a $14M commitment, primarily because the fear of "weak middle entries" is statistically overblown.

### 1. The Two-Window Comparison (Loudly Stated)
The brief requires us to look at this both ways, and the recommendation holds in both windows:

*   **All Years:** The median franchise bundle returns **4.63x**, while the median standalone returns **4.00x**. 
*   **2000 Onward:** The gap remains. Franchise bundles return **4.08x**, while standalones return **3.49x**. 

**Does the recommendation change between windows? No.** The winner on the median is the franchise bundle in both views. The only thing that changes is the shortlist: the best recent standalones (like *Spirited Away* or *Inception*) do not overlap at all with the best historical standalones (like *Jaws* or *Pulp Fiction*). 

### 2. The "Weak Middle Entry" Defense
The reason half the team fears bundles is the assumption that you are overpaying for the bad movies to get the good ones. Our data proves this is not the case for the top targets.
*   Even the **weakest** film in *The Lord of the Rings* bundle returned **8.37x**.
*   The weakest film in the next-best bundle (*Ice Age*) returned **5.50x**.
*   If we can secure a bundle where the *floor* is 8.37x, we are beating the typical standalone film (which averages 4.00x) even if the worst movie flops.

### 3. The Shortlist to Negotiate From
Kwame asked for three franchise targets and five standalone targets. 

**Top 3 Franchise Targets (Ranked by their weakest film to protect the floor):**
1.  **The Lord of the Rings Collection:** 3 films, 8.37x worst-film ROI, 9.96x combined ROI.
2.  **Ice Age Collection:** 3 films, 5.50x worst-film ROI, 7.43x combined ROI.
3.  **The Avengers Collection:** 4 films, 4.98x worst-film ROI, 6.13x combined ROI.

**Top 5 Standalone Hits (2000 Onward - the most relevant window):**
1.  **Spirited Away** (13.47x ROI)
2.  **The Incredibles** (5.86x ROI)
3.  **Inception** (4.24x ROI)
4.  **The Smurfs** (4.12x ROI)
5.  **Monsters, Inc.** (4.00x ROI)

### 4. Talent Leverage
When negotiating, our data shows that **directors** are a stronger signal of reliable ROI than lead actors. In the 2000-onward window, a director-led franchise is a safer bet than relying on an ensemble cast. (Specific talent metrics are available in the pipeline output).
