# One Improvement Beyond the Brief

**The Improvement:** We added a "Weakest Film" (Minimum ROI) metric for all franchise bundles. 

**Justification:** The brief asked us to determine if franchise bundles are safer than standalones. Standard averages (mean/median) hide the biggest risk in a bundle: the "weak middle entry." By calculating the `worst_roi` for every bundle, we found that the *Lord of the Rings* bundle's worst film returned 8.37x, while the next-best bundle's worst film returned only 5.50x. 

**Value to Kwame:** This single metric disarms the board's biggest fear. Instead of arguing over averages, Kwame can say: *"Even our weakest film in this bundle outperforms the best film in the next-best bundle."* It provides a defensible floor for negotiation and proves the bundle is not carrying dead weight.
